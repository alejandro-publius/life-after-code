# Discovery: GitLab and Google Cloud hackathons, January 2025 to October 6, 2026

Research for Alex Velazquez (solo entrant in Life After Code, the GitLab Transcend Hackathon). Checked on 2026-10-06. Goal: list the past hackathons whose winners we should study.

## Read this first

- Devpost (every page), about.gitlab.com, forum.gitlab.com, handbook.gitlab.com, docs.gitlab.com, contributors.gitlab.com, Kaggle, lablab.ai, mlh.io, zenn.dev, discuss.google.dev and most news sites were blocked by the proxy. See "Blocked".
- The web search budget (shared by all agents in this session) ran out after my 11th search. After that I only used sources I could open: the gitlab.com API (public issues, merge requests, repositories, groups) and cloud.google.com (blog posts, the "What's new in Google Cloud" logs, event pages, site maps). Several leads therefore stay open.
- Labels used below:
  - **(search excerpt)**: text from a search engine summary of a page I could not open. Likely right, not checked.
  - **(participant README)**: a claim in an entrant's public GitLab repository. It shows the event existed. It is not an official rule.
  - **(unverified)**: a lead that no source I could open confirms.
- **Relevance** (High, Medium, Low) is judged against our Path A project: an agent on GitLab that automates post-code work (review, security, testing, deployment, monitoring), with a human approving anything that matters, judged by people from GitLab, Google and Anthropic ([AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md)).
- Rows marked **Covered elsewhere** are researched by other agents (see [google-adk-hackathon-2025.md](google-adk-hackathon-2025.md) and [google-cloud-run-hackathon.md](google-cloud-run-hackathon.md)). I give them one line each.

## Short answer

Best hackathons to study, most relevant first:

1. GitLab AI Hackathon, Feb 9 to Mar 25, 2026 (covered elsewhere).
2. GitLab Transcend Hackathon, June 10 to 24, 2026 (covered elsewhere).
3. **Google Cloud Rapid Agent Hackathon, GitLab Track** (deadline June 11, 2026). New find. GitLab was one of five partner prize buckets, and the rules ask for multi-step plans carried out "under user oversight" (search excerpt). Winners not found; someone should open the Devpost winners page in a normal browser.
4. Google Cloud AI in Action, GitLab Challenge, May 6 to June 17, 2025 (covered elsewhere).
5. **DevOps × AI Agent Hackathon 2026** (Google Cloud Japan sponsor). Best theme match outside GitLab: build an agent, run it through CI/CD, deploy it on Google Cloud. Winners not found.
6. **Gemini Live Agent Challenge** (2026) and **GKE Turns 10 Hackathon** (2025): Google Cloud agent hackathons with full public winner lists on cloud.google.com, which I could read.
7. **All Things Agentic Hackathon** (Aug 3 to 31, 2026, $190,000): the newest Google Cloud agent hackathon. Winners not found; judging may still be running.

GitLab's quarterly Community Hackathons are well documented, but they reward merged contributions to GitLab itself, not agent projects. Their "winners" are leaderboard ranks.
